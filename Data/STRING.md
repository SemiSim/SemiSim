
1. Input FASTA
2. Organism = Homo Sapiens
3. Click 'Search'
4. Click 'Exports'
5. From the 'Explore your current network' list of formats, find '...as tabular text output' & click 'Download'
6. Open download with notepad/other text editor
7. Rename to `proteinID_string` & DELETE THE '.tsv' FROM THE NAME
8. Save as .txt file (UTF-8)
